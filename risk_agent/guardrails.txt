RISK GUARDRAILS POLICY
Owned by: Risk & Compliance team. Edit this file to change risk policy --
no code deployment required, RiskAgent reads it fresh on every run.

TIER DEFINITIONS

HIGH risk if any of the following hold:
- 2 or more counterparty trend deviations detected in the same run.
- Missed payments affect 20% or more of active counterparties.
- Any single counterparty has more than one unusually high-value
  transaction flagged in the same run.

MEDIUM risk if none of the HIGH conditions apply, but any of the following hold:
- At least 1 counterparty trend deviation.
- At least 1 unusually high-value transaction.
- Missed payments affect more than 5% but less than 20% of active counterparties.

LOW risk if none of the above apply:
- No deviations, no high-value flags, and missed payments affect 5% or
  fewer of active counterparties.

REASONING REQUIREMENTS

- Always name the specific counterparties driving the highest concern,
  not just the counts.
- If a deviation is a decrease rather than an increase, note that this
  may indicate reduced business activity or a churn risk rather than an
  AML concern, and should be weighed differently than an increase.
- Do not recommend specific actions (e.g. "freeze this account") -- this
  agent assesses risk tier only; the Reminder and AML agents handle
  downstream action.

OUTPUT FORMAT

Respond in exactly this format:

RISK_TIER: <low|medium|high>
REASONING: <2-4 sentences citing the specific counterparties and figures
that drove the tier>
